# Decisions

From the Time Governance Automation Plan (2026-10-01) and the owner's answers
to it. Change one only deliberately, and record the change here.

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Why                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **A standalone app**, not a module of Clarity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Clarity is scoped as an AR & collections app; this keeps its scope, spec and subprocessor ledger unchanged. Patterns (Karbon seam, send claims, brand) are reused, not shared code. |
| 2   | **Billability, PTO and sick come from a Task Types tab** in the roster workbook.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Karbon marks billability on the task type, but `GET /v3/IndividualTimeEntries` returns only the task type's name. Unclassified types are reported weekly.                           |
| 3   | **Minimal week = under 20 h** worked for a full-time week; scaled by Expected Weekly Hours; reduced in proportion to weekday firm holidays and PTO/sick logged. A whole week off is not checked. Someone hired mid-week is not checked for missing time that week.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Approved default (decision 1).                                                                                                                                                      |
| 4   | **Utilization = billable ÷ capacity**; capacity = Karbon `CapacityMinutesPerWeek` × working days ÷ 5, net of firm holidays, gross of PTO; fallback roster hours → 40 h; mid-month hires measured from hire date. Target = roster column.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Approved default (decision 4). The sample email's figures were placeholders.                                                                                                        |
| 5   | **Ad hoc = time on a client's Ad Hoc work item** (one exists on every client, found by title). Flag over 4 h for one client in a week, or the same person + client 3 weeks running with at least 1 h each week (owner, 2026-10-01: 15 minutes a week is not a pattern). Ad hoc time on the internal client is not checked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Owner's answer + approved default (decision 5).                                                                                                                                     |
| 6   | **Both weekly emails cover the previous Monday–Sunday.** Tuesday flags it; Friday re-checks it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Approved default (decision 6).                                                                                                                                                      |
| 7   | **Recipients:** one Tuesday email per CSA slot (that slot's staff only), one Friday email to all Partners together, one monthly email per Manager Email. Plus an admin summary to `TG_ADMIN_TO`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Approved default (decision 7). The admin summary keeps roster hygiene out of the people-facing emails.                                                                              |
| 8   | **Exclusions:** Exclude From Checks = Yes, or Status = Inactive, skips a person everywhere.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Approved default (decision 8).                                                                                                                                                      |
| 9   | **Friday escalates only people the Tuesday review flagged** for missing/minimal time and who are still missing. If Tuesday did not run for that week, everyone still missing is escalated and the admin summary says why.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The SOP escalates after CSAs have followed up; a skipped Tuesday shouldn't silence Friday.                                                                                          |
| 10  | **"Weeks flagged" counts escalations** in the last 4 weeks (this week included). Until 4 weeks of history exist, the email says when history starts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Partners act on repeat delinquency, not on a first miss.                                                                                                                            |
| 11  | **Shadow mode first; sends claimed before sending.** `email_sends` has a unique (kind, period, recipient, mode) key: re-runs and double fires never duplicate; a definitive failure releases the claim; an interrupted send stays "in doubt" and is not auto-retried.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Same posture as Clarity's send claims — a record never claims a send that didn't happen, and nothing double-sends.                                                                  |
| 12  | **Brand:** `#BA2025` (the firm's mark, Clarity ADR-0057) in the top bar only; tables use a charcoal header; status reds are tinted pills/text. The samples' `#8B1A1A` is retired.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Brand red and alarm red share a hue; they are separated by treatment.                                                                                                               |
| 13  | **No web UI in v1.** The roster is uploaded with `pnpm tg roster:import`; runs are inspected with `pnpm tg status`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Keeps v1 small and avoids a second login surface. Revisit if non-technical staff need to upload.                                                                                    |
| 14  | ~~History retention 400 days~~ — superseded by 19.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 15  | **The internal client is 99999**, set by its client ID (`KARBON_INTERNAL_CLIENT_IDS`) and resolved to Karbon's key at the start of each run via the organization / contact / client-group lookups. Anything billable on it is flagged (check 1). Ad hoc time on it is not checked (check 4): it is administrative, not client work to reassign.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Owner, 2026-10-01: "scanning 99999 … anything billable on this client should be flagged." Configuring by the firm's own ID avoids hunting for Karbon's internal key.                |
| 16  | **CSA emails list the entries behind each indicator flag** — date, client, hours, task type; the role for check 2; the description for checks 3 and 4 — up to five per flag, then "+N more".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Owner, 2026-10-01. A count alone sends the CSA hunting through Karbon before they can follow up.                                                                                    |
| 17  | **The manager report shows the month-over-month trend.** Last month is recomputed from Karbon in the same run (same capacity, same rules) rather than read from stored history, so it works from the first report and reflects any late corrections. The change is taken from the rounded percentages so it matches the two numbers shown. A prior month with no time logged is "nothing to compare", not 0% (first month in Karbon, e.g. the Tax practice's move from Axcess). The summary names people under target with their trend, plus anyone who dropped `UTILIZATION_DROP_POINTS` (10) or more while still at target. | Owner, 2026-10-01. A trend separates a slow month from a slide.                                                                                                                     |
| 18  | **No separate timeliness check.** The cadence is the timeliness check: Tuesday flags a week not entered by the day after Karbon's Monday reminder, Friday escalates it, and "weeks flagged" shows who is chronically late. Entering a week's time on the following Monday is what the SOP allows, so it is not flagged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Owner, 2026-10-01: keep expectations reasonable.                                                                                                                                    |
| 19  | **History is kept ~7 years but not reported.** `HISTORY_RETENTION_DAYS` defaults to 2557 (0 = keep forever). The emails only ever look back 4 weeks and the current year; anything older is reachable through `pnpm tg history`. Each week's result snapshots the person's name, department and manager, so history stays readable after they leave the roster.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Owner, 2026-10-01: "keep more than 13 months, just not report it." 7 years matches typical firm record retention.                                                                   |
| 20  | **Partner email shows the year-to-date count** ("3 of last 4 · 9 this year"), and 3+ escalations in a calendar year counts as a pattern even when not recent. `pnpm tg history` gives one person's record; `--export YYYY` gives a year's workbook for reviews.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Owner, 2026-10-01. The 4-week window alone hides a habit that comes and goes.                                                                                                       |

## Open follow-ups

- **Realization.** The SOP mentions it; time entries carry `BilledStatus` and
  rates, so a later report could show worked vs. billed.
- The SOP's "training codes on first-year clients" rule could become a fifth
  check; it needs a first-year flag per client.
- Calibrate `MINIMAL_WEEK_HOURS` (20 h) and the 10-character description test
  against the first dry runs on real data.
- Department-specific time-entry guides (SOP, "planned as a follow-on").
